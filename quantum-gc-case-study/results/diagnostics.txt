E1 aliasing:
  aliasing violation at [1] `cx x0 -> x0`: qubit x0 appears twice in one operation; QR-IR qubits are linear, so an operation cannot use one qubit in two roles
E2 control modified while ancilla live:
  cannot uncompute ancilla t0: its control x0 is modified by `h x0` at [5] while t0 is still needed at [6]
E3 unreclaimable (entangled) ancilla:
  ancilla t0 can never be reclaimed: `h t0` at [2] puts t0 in superposition, and it is not measured. Leaving it allocated would leave garbage entangled with the program state.
